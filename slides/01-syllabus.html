What Is This Course About?

Regression helps us understand and quantify relationships between variables.

By the end of the course, you should be able to

  • fit and interpret linear regression models,
  • make statistical inferences and predictions,
  • diagnose violations of model assumptions,
  • identify influential observations and multicollinearity,
  • select and compare useful models, and
  • analyze and communicate results using R.

Main Topics

  • Simple linear regression
  • Multiple linear regression
  • Least squares and matrix formulation
  • Estimation and inference
  • Prediction
  • Analysis of variance
  • Residual diagnostics
  • Transformations
  • Polynomial and categorical predictors
  • Outliers, leverage, and influence
  • Multicollinearity
  • Model selection and validation
